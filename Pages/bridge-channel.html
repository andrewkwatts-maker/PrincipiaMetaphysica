They are now one structure, closed by a single stated assumption. This page reports what the enumeration in AutoGenerated/topological_flux.json actually establishes, and what it does not.

2. What the enumeration forces

Enumerating every labelling of the 7 Fano lines by 3 blocks (37 in total) gives two results that were previously carried as inputs.

Now derived nfaces = 4 is forced.

4 is the maximum number of Fano points that can be simultaneously "rainbow" — that is, have all three lines through them carrying three distinct blocks. No labelling makes five or more points rainbow, so the face count cannot be raised. Previously this was read off h1,1 = 4 of TCS #187, a value the framework itself classifies FITTED.

Now derived Genericity is a consequence, not a criterion.

    6. What this does not settle

    Still open
    • The premise is an input. The join is conditional on the E8 block being a property of the channel rather than of the observing face. Nothing derives that; it is recorded as an assumption in the artifact and repeated as one here.
    • The uniqueness claim is conditional. If anything elsewhere in the framework distinguishes a Fano direction — a preferred imaginary octonion, a shadow asymmetry singling out one coordinate — PSL(3,2) is broken, the seven arcs stop being equivalent, and the choice of arc becomes physical again.
    • The flux integral is flat-space only. —
    • The TCS obstruction is untouched and still open. (b2, b3) = (4, 24) cannot exist as a twisted connected sum: Crowley–Nordström parity forces b2 + b3 odd, and 4 + 24 = 28. b3 = 24 survives — Joyce 1996 has (7, 24) — so it is b2 = 4, the "four faces" reading, that fails on the TCS side. Deriving nfaces = 4 from the block labelling removes the framework's dependence on h1,1 = 4 of TCS #187, but it does not repair the TCS construction itself.
