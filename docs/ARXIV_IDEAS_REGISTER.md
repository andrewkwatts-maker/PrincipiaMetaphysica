# arXiv Ideas Register

**Dated 2026-08-19.** This document is an ideas register for the outstanding problems in the Principia Metaphysica framework (26D bulk with signature (24,2), two 13D(12,1) shadows, G2-manifold compactification with b3 = 24). For each open issue — falsified predictions, underived assumptions, and speculative sectors — it lists real, verified arXiv papers whose specific mechanisms or measurements could either repair the prediction, supply the missing derivation, or sharpen the falsification. Every arXiv ID below was verified to exist via web search on the date above; none are invented. Entries prefer 2023-2026 literature where the experimental landscape moved (DESI DR2, NuFIT 6.0, Hyper-K) and foundational papers where the theoretical machinery originates.

## 1. Two-time physics: deriving the Sp(2,R) constraint

- arXiv:hep-th/0008164 — Survey of Two-Time Physics (Bars, 2000): the canonical statement that gauging Sp(2,R) acting on the (x,p) phase-space doublet is precisely what removes ghosts and forces the (d,2) signature — the target theorem our topology (b3 = 24) would need to reproduce.
- arXiv:hep-th/0605267 — Interacting Two-Time Physics Field Theory With a BRST Gauge Invariant Action (Bars et al., 2006): provides the BRST field-theoretic formulation of the Sp(2,R) constraint, giving a concrete action-level structure that a dimensional reduction from the 26D bulk would have to land on.
- arXiv:hep-ph/9910207 — Constraints on Extra Time Dimensions (Dvali, Gabadadze & Senjanović, 1999): works out the phenomenology of gravity propagating in extra times (screened Newtonian potential, tachyonic KK gravitons, imaginary self-energies) — the constraint set any (24,2) bulk must evade.
- arXiv:2312.02262 — Equivalence of EFTs and Time-Like Extra Dimensions (Hinterbichler & Saha, 2023): shows a brane in a two-time de Sitter background yields an EFT identical to a standard one-time AdS setup, a modern mechanism for how a second time can be physically present yet observationally hidden.

## 2. Cabibbo angle from geometry

- arXiv:2002.11820 — Quark and lepton mass matrices from localization in M-theory on G2 orbifold (Gonzalez, Kane, Nguyen & Perry, 2020): derives quark/lepton mass hierarchies and mixing from wavefunction localization at resolved E8 singularities of a G2 orbifold — the closest existing template for extracting lambda from our G2 geometry rather than from an exp(-3/2) ansatz.
- arXiv:2507.03076 — Reproducing Standard Model Fermion Masses and Mixing in String Theory: A Heterotic Line Bundle Study (Constantin, Leung, Lukas & Nutricati, 2025): demonstrates a string compactification whose moduli space contains points reproducing the measured quark masses and CKM mixing, showing moduli stabilization (cf. our racetrack T_min = 1.4885) is the correct place to look for lambda = 0.225.
- arXiv:2607.26681 — From Yukawa Couplings to Flavor Observables: A Top-Down, Analytically Solvable Geometric Framework for CKM/PMNS Mixing and CP Violation (Lin, 2026): builds the CKM matrix as pure geometric misalignment of flavor vectors, offering an analytic bridge from a geometric quantity to the Wolfenstein parameter.
- arXiv:2310.15604 — Radiative correction on moduli stabilization in modular flavor symmetric models (Kobayashi et al., 2023): shows how the stabilized modulus value (analogous to our racetrack e^{-T_min} = 0.2257 candidate) feeds directly and calculably into hierarchical Yukawa structures.

## 3. theta13 after the asin(1/6) falsification

- arXiv:1706.08749 — Are neutrino masses modular forms? (Feruglio, 2017): the foundational proposal that a single stabilized modulus VEV fixes all lepton masses and mixing angles — the natural replacement strategy for a one-off closed-form theta13 candidate.
- arXiv:2311.09282 — Neutrino Mass and Mixing with Modular Symmetry (Ding & King, 2023): comprehensive review cataloguing modular-symmetry models and their fitted theta13 values against global data, a shopping list for mechanisms that land at 8.5-8.6 deg instead of 9.59 deg.
- arXiv:2604.04585 — Predictions of Modular Symmetry Fixed Points on Neutrino Masses, Mixing, and Leptogenesis (Priya, Chauhan, Kumar & Nomura, 2026): shows that fixed points of the modulus and their neighborhoods produce viable neutrino phenomenology, i.e. small geometric deviations from symmetric points naturally shift theta13 by exactly the ~1 deg our candidate missed.
- arXiv:2506.02639 — Neutrino mass model at a three-loop level from a non-holomorphic modular A4 symmetry (2026): a current example of a non-holomorphic modular A4 construction fitted against NuFIT 6.0, the benchmark our falsified candidate must now be compared to.

## 4. Spectral index: replacing the n_s = 0.9996 variant

- arXiv:hep-th/0509012 — Kahler Moduli Inflation (Conlon & Quevedo, 2005): shows Kähler moduli generically support inflation with the model-independent prediction n_s = 1 - 2/N_e ~ 0.960-0.967, exactly the Planck window — the natural fix if our Re(T) potential is reshaped to their exponential form.
- arXiv:0808.0691 — Fibre Inflation: Observable Gravity Waves from IIB String Compactifications (Cicoli, Burgess & Quevedo, 2008): the fibre-modulus inflaton with n_s ~ 0.967 and r ~ 0.005, a concrete geometry-driven mechanism directly adaptable to a G2 modulus.
- arXiv:1311.0472 — Superconformal Inflationary alpha-Attractors (Kallosh, Linde & Roest, 2013): shows any potential becomes n_s = 1 - 2/N under a pole in the kinetic term — meaning the fix may lie in our Kähler metric for T, not in the superpotential.
- arXiv:1404.2601 — Inflation and String Theory (Baumann & McAllister, 2014): the standard monograph-level review mapping which string-moduli inflation mechanisms survive Planck, useful for triaging replacement candidates.

## 5. Dark energy: w0 = -23/24 vs DESI DR2

- arXiv:2503.14738 — DESI DR2 Results II: Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations and Cosmological Constraints (DESI Collaboration, 2025): the flagship DR2 result favoring evolving dark energy with w0 > -1, wa < 0 at 2.8-4.2 sigma over LCDM — the primary dataset our w0 = -0.9583 with evolving w(z) must be fitted against.
- arXiv:2404.05722 — Quintessential interpretation of the evolving dark energy in light of DESI (Tada & Terada, 2024): shows a quintessential scalar can reproduce the DESI-preferred w0wa track, providing the mapping from a fundamental-scalar w(z) to the CPL parameters DESI reports.
- arXiv:2408.17318 — Scant evidence for thawing quintessence (Wolf et al., 2024): demonstrates that DESI-compatible thawing models are prior-dominated and cluster near w0 ~ -1, defining exactly how much (or little) evidence currently backs a w0 ~ -0.96 claim.
- arXiv:2607.07777 — The Status of Single Scalar Field Dark Energy (García-García, Ferreira & Wolf, 2026): current global assessment finding quintessence only marginally distinguishable from Lambda, the honest statistical context for advertising -23/24 as a sharp prediction.

## 6. H0 tension: 71.55 canonical and the mirror sector

- arXiv:2103.01183 — In the Realm of the Hubble tension: a Review of Solutions (Di Valentino et al., 2021): the standard taxonomy of H0-tension solutions against which the framework's three variant values (70.42 / 71.55 / 76.34) can each be classified.
- arXiv:2107.13000 — Symmetry of Cosmological Observables, and a High Hubble Constant as an Indicator of a Mirror World Dark Sector (Cyr-Racine, Ge & Knox, 2021): shows a mirror dark sector scaling symmetry raises H0 while preserving CMB/LSS fits — the closest published mechanism to our shadow sector, including its known BBN-helium obstruction.
- arXiv:2302.09032 — The Ups and Downs of Early Dark Energy solutions to the Hubble tension (Poulin, Smith & Karwal, 2023): review of EDE models, hints, and constraints circa 2023, relevant if the framework's Euclidean information sector is to act before recombination.
- arXiv:2504.01669 — The CosmoVerse White Paper: Addressing observational tensions in cosmology with systematics and fundamental physics (Di Valentino et al., 2025): the current community-wide status of H0/S8 tensions, the reference point for quoting any sigma-level for 71.55 or 76.34.

## 7. Neutrino mass sum: 0.099 eV vs DESI

- arXiv:2404.03002 — DESI 2024 VI: Cosmological Constraints from the Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations (DESI Collaboration, 2024): the origin of the Sigma m_nu < 0.072 eV bound — but only under LCDM, an assumption the framework explicitly does not share.
- arXiv:2406.14554 — Neutrino mass bounds from DESI 2024 are relaxed by Planck PR4 and cosmological supernovae (Allali & Notari, 2024): shows the bound loosens to ~0.1-0.12 eV with Planck PR4 likelihoods and SNe, which alone re-admits our 0.099 eV prediction.
- arXiv:2407.10965 — Negative neutrino masses as a mirage of dark energy (Elbers et al., 2024): demonstrates that in w0wa/mirage dark-energy cosmologies (i.e. exactly the evolving-w world the framework predicts) the data favor larger neutrino masses consistent with laboratory constraints; note DESI DR2 itself quotes Sigma < 0.16 eV in the w0wa model (arXiv:2503.14738).

## 8. Proton decay: tau_p = 4.76e34 yr

- arXiv:hep-th/0211269 — Unification Scale, Proton Decay, And Manifolds Of G2 Holonomy (Friedmann & Witten, 2002): the definitive computation of GUT thresholds and proton decay in G2 compactifications, including the enhancement of p -> pi0 e+_L over other channels — the mechanism our channel-specific prediction should be checked against.
- arXiv:1805.04163 — Hyper-Kamiokande Design Report (Hyper-Kamiokande Proto-Collaboration, 2018): projects sensitivity reaching ~1e35 yr for p -> e+ pi0 over 20 years, meaning our 4.76e34 yr prediction sits squarely inside Hyper-K's discovery window.
- arXiv:1610.03597 — Search for Proton Decay via p -> e+ pi0 and p -> mu+ pi0 in 0.31 megaton-years exposure of Super-Kamiokande (Super-Kamiokande Collaboration, 2016): the current experimental floor (tau > 1.6e34 yr for e+ pi0), which our prediction already clears by a factor of ~3.

## 9. ALP at 3.51 meV

- arXiv:2012.06634 — Physics potential of the International Axion Observatory (IAXO) / Axion search with BabyIAXO in view of IAXO (Abeln et al., IAXO Collaboration, 2020): BabyIAXO reaches g_ag ~ 1.5e-11 GeV^-1 across meV masses — sitting essentially on top of our (3.51 meV, ~1e-11 GeV^-1) ansatz point.
- arXiv:2112.02286 — Digging into Axion Physics with (Baby)IAXO (Dafni & Galán, 2021): reviews the full (Baby)IAXO physics program in the meV-eV band, spelling out what a detection or exclusion at our benchmark would mean.
- arXiv:2504.00079 — The International Axion Observatory (IAXO): case, status and plans (IAXO Collaboration, 2025): current schedule and staged sensitivity, giving the realistic timeline on which the ALP ansatz becomes testable.
- arXiv:1206.0819 — The type IIB string axiverse and its low-energy phenomenology (Cicoli, Goodsell & Ringwald, 2012): shows moduli stabilization generically yields log-hierarchical axion masses with computable photon couplings — the template for deriving (rather than positing) a 3.51 meV ALP from the compactification.

## 10. Cross-shadow Bell correlations

- arXiv:1707.06050 — Spin Entanglement Witness for Quantum Gravity (Bose et al., 2017): the canonical protocol for certifying entanglement generated through an unobserved mediator — the experimental logic to adapt if the second time dimension is the mediator between sectors.
- arXiv:1707.06036 — Gravitationally-induced entanglement between two massive particles is sufficient evidence of quantum effects in gravity (Marletto & Vedral, 2017): proves entanglement generation certifies non-classicality of the mediating sector, the theorem needed to claim cross-shadow correlations would evidence a quantum second time.
- arXiv:0812.0210 — On determinism and well-posedness in multiple time dimensions (Craig & Weinstein, 2008): establishes when field equations with two times admit a well-posed initial-value problem, the mathematical precondition for any causal account of cross-shadow correlations.
- arXiv:2510.20991 — Absence of gravitationally induced entanglement in certain semi-classical theories of gravity (2025): a cautionary result showing plausible mediator theories generate no entanglement at all, sharpening what a cross-shadow Bell test could actually discriminate.
