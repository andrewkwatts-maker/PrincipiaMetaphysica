#### The selection argument gets SHARPER when the family becomes runnable

`compare_paths` used to report `both_pass_structurally = True` with the
reading "both paths give three generations, so the structural test does NOT
decide between them -- the ruling is genuinely the author's". With only two
paths declared that was correct. With the family runnable it is incomplete,
and the corrected statement is stronger:

**The structural test DOES select -- within the family.** It eliminates the
profiles giving 0, 1 and 2 generations and leaves exactly one. What it does
not do is separate (12, 43) from the off-family seed_24, which also gives
three via 24/8. That separation is REACHABILITY: seed_24 fails b_3 = 7 +
3 b_2, fails b_3 = 7 (mod 12), and sits below the exhibited TCS range.

So the adoption rests on TWO independent criteria, neither referencing a
measurement:

  generation count   selects uniquely WITHIN the reachable family
  reachability       excludes the off-family candidate that also gives three

`compare_paths` now reports both (`reachable_paths_passing_structural`,
`structural_test_is_decisive_within_the_family`,
`what_separates_the_two_that_pass`), and the tests assert each separately
so a future change that weakens one cannot hide behind the other.
