# Plan: close the G2 geometry on the adopted path (D-023), 2026-10-25

## What "closed" means here
No explicit compact G2 metric is known anywhere. Joyce's construction is closed when
every hypothesis of the existence theorem is measured on the adopted seed
(seed_43_joyce: T^7/Gamma, Gamma = (Z2)^3, b2 = 12, b3 = 43), each measurement can
fail, and the conclusions follow by citation:
- existence of a torsion-free G2 structure: Joyce 2000, Thm 11.6.1 (Joyce 1996 I, Thm A);
- Ricci-flat: torsion-free implies Ricci-flat (Bonan);
- Hol = G2: Joyce 1996 II, Prop 1.1.1, holonomy G2 iff pi_1 finite.
