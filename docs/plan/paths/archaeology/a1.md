Rewrites to a 13D (12,1) bulk, D3-brane, 9 compact dims on unspecified M9, 64-component non-chiral Cl(12,1) Pneuma, emergent thermal time (Tomita-Takesaki), Mashiach as radion, Myrzakulov F(R,T) attractor, SME gravitational-wave dispersion. 5. 2025-08-06 Wastepad 1 (QUT A-Q). Adds a thermal genesis story: M13 = M4 x K_Pneuma x T_thermal, SO(13)_thermal chain to SO(10), N_gen = 3 from a winding integral, Omega_m = 1/Pi_U, proton lifetime ~1e36 yr. (a1-v3)
6. 2025-08-06 Wastepad 2 (QUT math). Keeps (12,1) but adds Sp(2,R): G = SO(12,1) x SO(10) x Sp(2,R). Adds CDT, alpha-attractor inflation, cosmic strings, IIT/Orch-OR. (a1-v4)
7. 2025-08-06 Wastepad 3. Fundamental (14,2) with thermal gauge fixing to (12,1); merges the QUT with the 2T "Unified Framework". Scalar consciousness field, w(z) = -1 + 0.1 z/(1+z). (a1-v5)
8. 2025-08-09 SO(10) + 2T + consciousness report. A review-style document: generic (D,2) with Sp(2,R), conventional SO(10) (16, 45 generators, 45/54/16/126 Higgs), modular-flow time, spin-liquid consciousness. No Pneuma, no Mashiach, no numerics. (a1-v6)

Supersession, as far as the documents show: 2-time (10,2) was dropped for single-time on causality grounds (Analysis, (12,1) PDFs); two times returned in wastepads 2-3 and the Aug-9 report. The Adjustments PDF argues for the opposite choice from the Analysis PDF: it says Sp(2,R) constraints already preserve causality. The Aug-9 report drops the Pneuma/Mashiach vocabulary. No document marks anything retired; status_now values are the cataloguer's reading.

## Top 5 criticisms (from the Adjustments and Analysis PDFs, plus cataloguer checks where marked)

1. Existence and grounding. Both PDFs say Pneuma, Mashiach, U(1)_consciousness and the named adaptations are not in the literature (Analysis: "appears to be fictional or speculative"). Hits every branch. Both PDFs are AI-generated research output and are treated as data here.
2. Two times and causality. The original (10,2) signature gave "severe causality paradoxes" and the "multiple clock" problem (Analysis). The Adjustments PDF disagrees: Sp(2,R) constraints remove ghosts. Later versions oscillate between the two positions.
3. Quintessence and EFT costs. Mashiach as quintessence needs a very flat potential (|V''| << H^2), has the coincidence problem, and needs fifth-force screening. In 12D the beta functions run fast; Landau poles below the cutoff must be avoided (Analysis). Hits v1.
4. Anomaly cancellation for U(1)_consciousness may need extra particles (Analysis). Cataloguer note: wastepad 1 weakens the conditions with "modulo thermal corrections", and anomaly polynomials are an even-dimension notion, so the 13D claims need support.
5. Content missing. The (12,1) PDF defers M9, the KK spectrum, F(R,T) and V(M). Predicted magnitudes are 1/Lambda-suppressed with Lambda unspecified; Pi_U = pi and N_gen = 3 are inputs. 2T physics yields hidden symmetries, not the direct new-particle predictions PM claims (Analysis).

Cataloguer arithmetic flags (not from the sources, marked in entries): "49 orders of magnitude" is 58 for 1e-33 eV to 1e25 eV; the SO(10)/(SU(4)xSU(2)xSU(2)) coset has dimension 24, not 8; wastepad 3's slope 0.1 breaks wastepad 1's own |w+1| < 0.01 bound for z > 1.
